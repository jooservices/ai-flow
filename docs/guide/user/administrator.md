# User guide: Administrator

**Assumes:** you are signed in to Command Center with the `administrator` role (formerly Super Administrator or Owner) and AI Flow v4 is installed. You do not need a command line for any task on this page. The role table is in `docs/v4/role-based-views.md`.

## Your views

Every view: [Command Center](../screens/cc.md), [Work board](../screens/work.md), [Delivery](../screens/delivery.md), [SA health](../screens/sa.md), [Team Lead](../screens/lead.md), [Business](../screens/business.md), [Human review](../screens/review.md), [Stakeholder](../screens/stakeholder.md), [Machines and AI](../screens/machines.md), [Users and roles](../screens/users.md), [Knowledge](../screens/knowledge.md), [Integrations](../screens/integrations.md), [Projects](../screens/projects.md), [Backup](../screens/backup.md), [Licence](../screens/licensing.md) and [Notifications](../screens/notifications.md).

You land on the Dashboard after you sign in. If you hold several roles you land on the page of the first role in this order: Administrator, Product owner, Delivery, Team Lead, SA, BA, Business, Reviewer, Stakeholder.

## People and roles

On [Users and roles](../screens/users.md) you add people, give or remove roles, limit a person to chosen projects and disable a person. Every change is audited with your name. The last administrator cannot be removed or disabled. Use **View as** in the top bar to see exactly the views of any role without signing out.

## Daily tasks

1. Open [Command Center](../screens/cc.md) and check the incident panel. Each incident has a [runbook](../runbooks/index.md).
2. Open [SA health](../screens/sa.md) for node and account health.
3. Open [Integrations](../screens/integrations.md) to confirm Jira and GitHub are connected.
4. Open [Projects](../screens/projects.md) to add, pause, resume or remove a project.

Press **Help** in the top bar: it opens the page of the screen you are on.
