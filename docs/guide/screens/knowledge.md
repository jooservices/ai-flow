# Knowledge

**Assumes:** you are signed in with a role that has `knowledge.read`. Creating and editing needs `knowledge.manage`;
approving policies needs `knowledge.approve` (Owner and Super Administrator).

Knowledge (`/knowledge`) holds study cases, knowledge, skills, agents, guides and policies that AI workers read.

## What you see

- **Resources**: the list, with a filter by kind and project. Items marked general are offered to every project; project items never leave their project.
- **Create resource / Edit resource**: each save makes a new version; **History** shows the earlier ones.
- **Policy approvals**: policies wait here until an owner approves them.

## Common tasks

- Create a resource, save it, and check its history. Archive what is no longer true.
- Approve or reject a pending policy.
