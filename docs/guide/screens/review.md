# Human review

**Assumes:** you are signed in with any console role and you have the reviewer access token from your administrator.
The token is never shown in the documentation.

Human review (`/review`) lists the tickets that wait for a person before merge. It refreshes every 15 seconds.

## What you see

For each ticket: the pull request, the verify result, the facts needed to decide, and the Approve and Rework actions.

## Common tasks

1. Enter the reviewer access token. It is held only in the page until you close it.
2. Read the ticket and its pull request.
3. Press Approve, or write why in the box and press Rework.
