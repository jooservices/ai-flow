# User guide: SA

**Assumes:** you are signed in to Command Center with the `sa` role and AI Flow v4 is installed. You see only the views of your role; if a control is missing, your role does not allow it. This is not an error. The role table is in `docs/v4/role-based-views.md`.

## Your views

[Dashboard](../screens/cc.md), [Work board](../screens/work.md), [SA health](../screens/sa.md), [Machines and AI](../screens/machines.md), [Integrations](../screens/integrations.md) and [Knowledge](../screens/knowledge.md).

You land on SA health after you sign in. With several roles you see the union of their views and land on the page of the first role in the order Administrator, Product owner, Delivery, Team Lead, SA, BA, Business, Reviewer, Stakeholder.
