# Team Lead

**Assumes:** you are signed in with any console role and the project has epics in Jira that AI Flow has synced.

Team Lead (`/lead`) shows requirement coverage and progress for the epics of one project.

## What you see

- Epic progress tiles.
- **Requirement coverage**: whether the tasks of each epic cover its acceptance criteria. A warning means an epic has too few tasks for its criteria.
- Progress by epic and ticket.

## Common tasks

- Choose the project, read the coverage warnings and ask the team to add tasks.
- Export the progress report as Markdown, CSV or printable HTML.
