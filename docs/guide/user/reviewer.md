# User guide: Reviewer

**Assumes:** you are signed in to Command Center with the `reviewer` role and AI Flow v4 is installed. You see only the views of your role; if a control is missing, your role does not allow it. This is not an error. The role table is in `docs/v4/role-based-views.md`.

## Your views

[Dashboard](../screens/cc.md), [Work board](../screens/work.md) and [Human review](../screens/review.md).

You land on Human review after you sign in. On that screen you approve, send back or reject work with your own sign-in (the `review.actions` permission); no separate reviewer access token is needed. With several roles you see the union of their views and land on the page of the first role in the order Administrator, Product owner, Delivery, Team Lead, SA, BA, Business, Reviewer, Stakeholder.
