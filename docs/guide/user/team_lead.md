# User guide: Team Lead

**Assumes:** you are signed in to Command Center with the `team_lead` role (formerly Lead) and AI Flow v4 is installed. You see only the views of your role; if a control is missing, your role does not allow it. This is not an error. The role table is in `docs/v4/role-based-views.md`.

## Your views

[Dashboard](../screens/cc.md), [Work board](../screens/work.md), [Delivery](../screens/delivery.md), [Team Lead](../screens/lead.md), [Human review](../screens/review.md) and [Knowledge](../screens/knowledge.md).

You land on Team Lead after you sign in. With several roles you see the union of their views and land on the page of the first role in the order Administrator, Product owner, Delivery, Team Lead, SA, BA, Business, Reviewer, Stakeholder.

## A usual week

1. Open [Team Lead](../screens/lead.md), pick the project and check that every epic has enough tasks for its acceptance criteria.
2. Open [Delivery](../screens/delivery.md) and read "Needs attention".
3. Export the progress report (Markdown, CSV or printable HTML) for your stakeholders.
